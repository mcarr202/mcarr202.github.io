Michelle Carrera

Liberal Studies and Education Major

I am a second year college student studying two majors at the University of California, Riverside. I have been a wanting to become an elementary school teacher since I first entered a classroom in Preschool. My passion for teaching only grew more and more while continuing my education and I grew up knowing I wanted to stay in this environment no matter the difficulties I may encounter throughout the years. My teachers and family have always been supportive of my career choice which only made my decision more positive and my passion stronger. Being inside a classroom has always been my dream and I continue to gain experience in order for my goals to be reached.

I have always tried to put in my service in a classroom whenever I could. Since 2010 I have been part of a bible study group at my Catholic church where I was able to gain leadership roles due to the amount of time I have been a part of the group. I had the opportunity to teach children from 6-12 for an hour once a week about a specific person, story, or lesson from the bible which allowed them to gain more knowledge about our world, religion, and why things are the way they are according to our faith. I have also been an elementary school teacher aide in a fifth grade classroom for a year. I would go to the class every other day and help out the studetns with their work, grade tests, file papers, reorganize the class, or document points for each student. Lastly, I was a counselor for nine girls for a week at a science-nature camp known as SCICON. I made sure they participated, listened to rules, learned about nature and how science plays a major role in it. I was also in charge of a tour group, making sure students stayed in line, payed attention, and were safe while we explored the camp during trails each day. Being a part of these experiences helped me gain social skills and made my passion for teaching greater, allowing me to be prepared for college and my future altogether.

I have had the honor to have my first job at an elementary school. Since February I have been a program leader at an after school program that takes place at an elementary school. I am in charge of twenty students in second and third grade where we go through different components each day. We start off with signing in during supper, move to homework and centers, go outside for some physical activity, and end the day with some science or art. It has been a wonderful experience connecting with students and watching them grow while they learn different subjects and participate in many activities. Being in charge of these students help me gain responsibility skills, keeping them safe and making sure they have fun in the process. This job makes me excited for my future career and has me prepared for what's to come.
